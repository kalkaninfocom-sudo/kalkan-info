Über uns

A coastal table where flavours travel

At the heart of Kalkan, Coast Tapas & Grill brings together the Mediterranean, Anatolia and the world on a single table. From Peruvian tiradito to open-fire grills, from fresh seafood to handcrafted pasta — every plate is prepared with seasonal produce and quiet craftsmanship.

We grow and source with care — Lures Garden tomatoes, our own cold-pressed olive oil, Ezine cheese — so every bite tastes of this region. Come for lunch by the light, stay for a long evening.
