About Us

Kalkan Dive Centre

Kalkan Dive Centre is a water sports facility located in Kalkan, a district of Kaş in Antalya, on Kalamar Beach. Our facility offers various water sports activities to its guests.

We offer various services for those who want to experience sea pleasure. With our experienced staff and safe equipment, we ensure that our customers have a safe and enjoyable experience.
