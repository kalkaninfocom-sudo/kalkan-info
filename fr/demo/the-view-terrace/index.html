About the Restaurant

Where the Aegean
meets the sky

Perched above the turquoise bay of Kalkan, The View Terrace is more than a meal — it is an atmosphere. The open terrace unfolds before you like a stage, with the old town's whitewashed rooftops cascading to the sea below and the sky above painted in every shade the Mediterranean sun can conjure.

Guests from across the world return season after season — drawn not only by the food, but by the rare feeling of a place that makes time slow down. A 4.9-star rating across 158 reviews is not an accolade, it is a pattern of moments.
