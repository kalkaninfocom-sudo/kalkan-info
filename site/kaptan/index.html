About Us
A story of sea & flavour

Captain's Table by the Sea

Kaptan Restaurant sits where Kaş whispers to the Aegean, our sunset terrace a front-row seat to liquid gold horizons. Here, families gather and lovers linger as the day surrenders to starlight.

Our daily catch arrives from local fishermen's boats—grilled to perfection, dressed simply with lemon and olive oil. Each mezze plate tells stories of Mediterranean traditions, shared slowly, savored deeply.
